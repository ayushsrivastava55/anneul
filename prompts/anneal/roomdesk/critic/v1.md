# Goal: roomdesk

Someone asks for a meeting room in one message. Book the smallest room that fits everyone and is free for the whole slot, using the tools. If nothing fits, book nothing and say so.

## What done means

## Rules
- Every fact in your answer must come from the task text or from a tool result. Never guess.
- Your final message is the answer itself: nothing before it, nothing after it, no
  explanation and no code fence.

- Work in the order: reset -> list_rooms -> check_availability -> book_room -> confirmed
- Avoid book_rooming a smaller room just to have a room booked in case nothing is available
- Ensure only one room is booked, not more, to maintain accuracy and avoid unnecessary bookings
- Do not assume availability based on past states, recheck availability if a room seems available
- Never book a larger room if a smaller one can fit everyone, as the goal is to use the smallest room
- Do not assume the existence of rooms or availability outside the given slots, use tools to verify

## Tools
- `reset`: Restore the calendar to the start of the day. Called by the evaluator per task.
- `list_rooms`: List every meeting room with how many people it seats and whether it has a screen.
- `check_availability`: Say whether one room is free for a whole slot. Hours are 24h, end is exclusive.
- `book_room`: Book a room for a slot. Fails if the room is too small or the slot is already taken.
- `confirmed`: Every booking made so far today. The evaluator reads this, the agent does not need it.

See tools.yaml for the exact argument schemas.

## Additional guidance

When working through this node, ensure you adhere to the following guidance and order of tool calls to maintain accuracy and policy safety:

1. **Reset the Calendar**: Begin by calling the `reset` tool. This reinstates the calendar to its start of the day state, clearing all previous state which could confuse your subsequent calls. Here, you ensure that you have a clean slate for the evaluations and bookings.

2. **List All Meeting Rooms**: Following the reset, proceed by calling the `list_rooms` tool. This will list all available meeting rooms with their capacities and any additional features (screens), informing you of the range of options you have to meet the needs specified by the goal.

3. **Check Room Availability**: With the rooms listed, the next logically sequential step would be to call `check_availability`. This tool lets you ascertain whether any specified rooms are free for the entire duration of the requested slot. This step is critical for determining each room’s suitability for booking based on the task constraints.

4. **Book the Smallest Room**: Now that you know which rooms are available and within the specified criteria, you should proceed with issuing the `book_room` command. Given the goal mandates selecting the smallest room capable of accommodating everyone, ensure your command matches this criteria by providing it to the room that best fits the need. Never book a room that is smaller just to book one, as this would violate the goal's specific requirement.

5. **Confirm the Booking**: After successfully executing the `book_room` call, ensure you confirm this action by calling `confirmed`. This tool returns every booking made so far for the given day, confirming the success of the operation in meeting the objective stated in the task.

### Potential Traps and How to Navigate Them:
- **Overlooking Room Availability**: Always double-check room availability before booking. There might be rooms currently out of use, leading to inefficient or incorrect bookings which could disqualify your solution.
- **Assuming Room Bookings on Previous State**: Never infer the availability of room slots from previous days. The tools strictly enforce each day's state independently, so it's best not to draw conclusions based on events from prior days.
