The loop

The same six steps whatever the job is. You watch them happen.

Design

Turns your goal and your tools into a few candidate agents.

Try it

Runs every design over your examples and scores each one.

Find the faults

Reads the failed attempts and groups them by what went wrong.

Fix one

Applies the repair that fault calls for. It never retries a spent one.

Prove it helped

Re-runs both versions on tasks held back from every earlier step.

Make it cheaper

Moves the winner onto cheaper models while the score holds.

Run it yourself

No account anywhere. The models run on your machine.
